# Complete invitation film templates

The November 2026 correction replaces the short-demo emphasis with three complete invitation films that follow the owner's supplied narrative references. Each full preview is rendered through the same scene compositor used by the editor, with its own fictional sample names and event details. The film files are complete invitation examples with music, not the earlier 10-second background demonstrations.

| Template | Length | Story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shaadi Ka Ghar | 52 seconds | Haveli opening, couple welcome, family blessings, functions, venue, closing namaste invitation |
| The Royal Door | 40 seconds | Real door opening, garden family reveal, names/date, ceremony itinerary, final invitation |
| Padharo, Sa! | 45 seconds | Rajasthan street/rickshaw and scroll, family invitation, golden fort, individual functions, illuminated evening welcome |

All three are in the homepage Complete Reels section, with Watch full reel and Use this reel actions. The full player includes audio, seeking, chapter navigation and a direct editor action. The preview has sample names; the editor starts with empty personal names, date and venue. Function names are editable. Adding a photo inserts a photo scene before the finale. Reordering scenes recalculates their weighted timings while keeping the chosen total film length.

Each template uses three original animated scene clips with independent editable text and scene transitions. Longer text scenes gently slow an ambient clip instead of looping a sudden cut. The haveli welcome uses compact top typography and holds its source framing before the close camera push can overlap the names. Users may adjust duration, typography, accent, music, functions, venue, RSVP, scene order and photos. Original template characters remain part of the scene footage. When the separate personal AI connection is configured, a generated character scene can replace the Meet the stars chapter without removing the remaining full film.

Full film previews: `public/full-films/{haveli-celebration,royal-garden,rajasthan-journey}/full-reel.mp4`. Source scenes and stills are stored beside them. Character/style provenance and exact prompts are in `haveli-full-film-assets.md`, `royal-garden-film-assets.md` and `rajasthan-full-film-assets.md`. The original score is documented in `full-film-score.md`.

The existing two-free-reel account limit, private exports and cart remain in effect. Shipped full templates work without a live AI API key. Personal AI scene generation and the owner-deferred payment checkout retain their separate connection states.
