# Custom AI film and own audio

Haveli Namaste is a 10-second Custom AI scene inspired by the supplied painted-haveli/namaste film. The public sample is the original supplied reference, optimized for the site. The editor renders each customer's private generated motion full-frame with editable invitation text. Switching from a standard template clears its active AI output, but keeps saved results in the account library.

## Credits

A new complete image-plus-motion generation requires an explicit five-credit confirmation and a sufficient balance. The atomic reservation prevents overspending and duplicate parallel requests. Standard avatar creation remains one credit. The two free credits therefore cover standard work, not a new five-credit custom film. Saved results and subsequent audio/text edits on the same project reuse its paid generation. Credit packs remain INR 299/3, 799/10, and 1499/25; checkout remains unavailable until the owner connects payment. The separate INR 99 custom-design consultation is unchanged.

## Own soundtrack

The Custom AI editor accepts a user-owned or authorized recording up to 60 seconds / 12 MB, subject to browser decoding support. It normalizes to 24 kHz mono PCM WAV, uploads to private account storage, and verifies the real format server-side. Uploads are deduplicated per owner. Audio metadata, trim position and volume persist with the draft. Preview and browser export mix the trimmed recording; no provider receives the recording. This is soundtrack mixing, not lip synchronization. The original instrumental remains available when no custom audio is chosen.

No new paid provider generation was run for this release.
